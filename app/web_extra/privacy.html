Privacy policy

"NovaSTEM: science & tech" app — last updated: 21 October 2026

Version française (the French version prevails in case of discrepancy)

In short

  • NovaSTEM requires no account and collects no name, email address, location or contacts.
  • Your choices (fields, favorites, theme, language, notification settings) stay on your phone.
  • There are no ads and no sale of data. Anonymous usage counters (with no identifier at all) and crash reports help us improve the app.
  • If you turn on notifications, an anonymous technical identifier is used by Google's notification service (Firebase).

1. Who publishes NovaSTEM?

NovaSTEM is published by Maxime Faucogney, under the developer name Jupiter Dynamics, an individual based in France.

Contact: jd136413@gmail.com

2. Data stored on your phone

The app keeps on your device, and only on your device: the fields chosen at first launch, your favorites (with their content, so you can read them offline), light or dark theme, language and notification settings. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone settings.

3. Loading breakthroughs

To display breakthroughs, the app reads a database hosted by the provider Supabase. This reading is anonymous: no personal identifier is sent. As with any Internet connection, the provider may record technical data (IP address, time of the request) in its operation and security logs; we do not use them to identify you.

4. Notifications (if you turn them on)

Notifications are off by default. If you turn them on in Settings → Notifications, the app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service. Your phone then receives a technical installation identifier (token) and subscribes to "topics" matching your choices (for example: Space, importance 4 and above). These subscriptions contain no personal information and do not reveal who you are.

To stop: turn off the switch in Settings → Notifications, or remove the permission in your phone settings. Google's processing is described in its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4a. Anonymous statistics

To learn which fields interest users most and improve the app, it sends anonymous counters to our database (hosted by Supabase): app opened, breakthrough opened (with its field and rating), favorite added, breakthrough or source hidden, share, notification choice, app language. These counters contain no identifier of the phone or the person (no name, email address, advertising ID or installation ID): it is impossible to know who did what, or to link two actions together. Only totals are viewed.

4b. Crash reports

If the app closes because of an error, a technical report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics (Google): error description, phone model and version, app version, and a technical installation identifier used to count crashes. These reports contain no name, email address or personal content. They are used only to fix errors and are kept for 90 days by Google (firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

4c. Google Play reviews

After several uses, the app may show Google Play's official window inviting you to rate the app. This window is managed by Google; we only receive the public rating and comment you choose to leave.

5. Article translation

On Android and iPhone, titles and summaries are translated directly on your phone with Google ML Kit. Translated text is not sent over the Internet. The first time a language is used, its translation model is downloaded from Google's servers.

6. Content produced by artificial intelligence

Breakthroughs are selected, summarized and ranked automatically by artificial intelligence (Claude, by Anthropic) from articles published by scientific and technology sources. This processing happens on our servers, before publication, and uses no data about you. Summaries may contain errors: the source article, available through the "Read the article" button, always prevails.

7. Links to other websites

The "Read the article" button opens the source's website in your browser. That website applies its own privacy policy, over which we have no control.

8. Children

NovaSTEM is intended for a general audience and collects no personal data, including from children.

9. Your rights

Since we hold no data that identifies you, we cannot in practice find you in our systems. You may still write to us with any question about your data or to exercise your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (access, rectification, erasure, objection): jd136413@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

10. Changes

This policy may change, for example if a new feature is added. The update date appears at the top of this page. Any significant change will be announced in the app.
